About me

I'm a senior in Business & Supply Chain Analytics at the Sirindhorn International Institute of Technology (SIIT). My interest sits where operations meet data: finding the manual, paper-based or copy-paste steps in a process and replacing them with tools that are faster and less error-prone.

During my internship in ECCO Thailand's Material Requirements Planning (MRP) team, I worked daily with SAP, purchase orders and raw-material requirements for shoe production, and built Excel VBA macros and Power BI dashboards that the team used in its daily work.
